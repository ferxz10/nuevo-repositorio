## Post 1 – [Two months ago]

[Link to original post] (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/zainkahn_the-biggest-shift-in-2026-isnt-technology-activity-7448339129781637120-VsU9?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAEgnkiABZdlJeQGmC1R-m2Hd8MQmVjYK_os)

"The biggest shift in 2026 isn’t technology.

It’s leverage.

I went through the AI predictions report from You.com Co-Founders Richard Socher and Bryan McCann and one thing stood out:

We’re not just getting better tools.
We’re getting more output per person.

What this looks like:

1. Bigger output, smaller teams
AI handles execution.

2. Less doing, more deciding
Value shifts to what and why.

3. Work = orchestration
Direct → review → decide.

4. New interface
From clicking tools → to telling systems.

The real takeaway:
The edge won’t come from better tools.

It’ll come from people who can:
• structure problems
• guide AI
• make faster decisions

Because leverage compounds.

We learned how to do the work.
Now we need to learn how to direct it.

2026 won’t be defined by AI alone.

It’ll be defined by the people who learn how to work with it properly."
