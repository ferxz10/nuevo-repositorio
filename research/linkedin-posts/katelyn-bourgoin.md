## Post 2 – [A week ago]

[Link to original post] (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/katebour_how-do-you-sell-a-product-that-solves-a-problem-activity-7467898892538417153-PhyA?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAEgnkiABZdlJeQGmC1R-m2Hd8MQmVjYK_os)

"How do you sell a product that solves a problem people don’t realize they have?

Smart packaging. 

Oftentimes people hunt for solutions to the symptoms of their problems. 

Symptoms can look like:

👉 ads that don’t convert
👉 teams that aren’t aligned
👉 social posts that get ignored 

These are symptoms of deeper problems. But your target customer may not know what their root problem is. Yet they’re painfully aware of the symptoms.

So you have two options:

Option 1: you educate them on the problem—helpful but can also be slooooooooooow.

Option 2: you reframe your message to sell them what they know they want and then deliver what they actually need.

I call this the Trojan Horse method.

And it works."
